# Code of conduct

[简体中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) | **English**

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: focus on the issue, not the person; respect different experiences and viewpoints; harassment and personal attacks are not accepted.
You can report violations to the maintainers through the repository's private reporting channel; reports are handled confidentially.
